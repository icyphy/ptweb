Dataflow Papers

Although this is neither up-to-date nor comprehensive, below is a list of selected papers about dataflow.

  1. Edward A. Lee and Eleftherios Matsikoudis, "The Semantics of Dataflow with Firing," Chapter in From Semantics to Computer Science: Essays in memory of Gilles Kahn, GÈrard Huet, Gordon Plotkin, Jean-Jacques LÈvy, Yves Bertot, editors, Preprint Version, March 07, 2008, Copyright (c) Cambridge University Press, 2008.
  2. Praveen K. Murthy and Edward A. Lee, "Multidimensional Synchronous Dataflow," IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, volume 50, no. 8, pp. 2064-2079, August 2002.
  3. Shuvra S. Bhattacharyya, Praveen K. Murthy, and Edward A. Lee, "Synthesis of Embedded Software from Synchronous Dataflow Specifications," Journal of VLSI Signal Processing Systems, Vol. 21, No. 2, June 1999.
  4. P. K. Murthy, S. S. Bhattacharyya, and E. A. Lee, "Joint Minimization of Code and Data for Synchronous Dataflow Programs, Journal of Formal Methods in System Design, Vol. 11, No. 1, pp41-70, July 1997.
  5. S. Ha and E. A. Lee, "Compile-Time Scheduling of Dynamic Constructs in Dataflow Program Graphs," IEEE Trans. on Computers, Vol. 46, No. 7, July 1997.
  6. S. S. Bhattacharyya, P. K. Murthy, and E. A. Lee, "APGAN and RPMC: Complimentary Heuristics for Translating DSP Block Diagrams into Efficient Software Implementations," Journal of Design Automation for Embedded Systems, Vol. 2, No. 1, pp. 33-60, January, 1997.
  7. W.-T. Chang, S.-H. Ha, and E. A. Lee, "Heterogeneous Simulation -- Mixing Discrete-Event Models with Dataflow," invited paper, Journal on VLSI Signal Processing, Vol. 13, No. 1, January 1997.
  8. S. S. Bhattacharyya, P. K. Murthy and E. A. Lee, "Software Synthesis from Dataflow Graphs," Kluwer Academic Publishers, Norwell, Mass, 1996.
  9. E. A. Lee and T. M. Parks, "Dataflow Process Networks," Proceedings of the IEEE, vol. 83, no. 5, pp. 773-801, May, 1995.
  10. J. L. Pino, S. Ha, E. A. Lee and J. T. Buck, "Software Synthesis for DSP Using Ptolemy," Journal on VLSI Signal Processing, vol. 9, no. 1, pp. 7-21, Jan., 1995.
  11. J. T. Buck, "Scheduling Dynamic Dataflow Graphs with Bounded Memory Using the Token Flow Model," Technical Memorandum UCB/ERL 93/69, Ph.D. Thesis, Dept. of EECS, University of California, Berkeley, CA 94720, 1993.
  12. E. A. Lee, "Consistency in Dataflow Graphs," IEEE Trans. on Parallel and Distributed Systems," vol. 2, no. 2, April 1991.
  13. E. A. Lee and D. G. Messerschmitt, "Synchronous Data Flow," Proc. of the IEEE, September, 1987.
  14. E. A. Lee and D. G. Messerschmitt, "Pipeline Interleaved Programmable DSPs: Synchronous Data Flow Programming," IEEE Trans. on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing, vol. ASSP-35(9), September, 1987.
  15. E. A. Lee and D. G. Messerschmitt, "Static Scheduling of Synchronous Data Flow Programs for Digital Signal Processing," IEEE Trans. on Computers, January, 1987.
