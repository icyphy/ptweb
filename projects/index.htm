The Ptolemy Projects

  • Research Projects - listed by student, which includes many of the projects below.
  • ActionWebs, Cyber-Physical Systems, funded by The NSF.
  • Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems (CHESS) industrial interactions (Denso, National Instruments, Toyota).
  • COSMOI - Compositional System Modeling with Interfaces, funded by the NSF.
  • Mathematical Theory of CPS, funded by the NFS.
  • Kepler - Scientific workflows.
  • iCyPhy Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems, funded by IBM and United Technologies.
  • PRET - Precision timed machines, funded by the NSF.
  • PTIDES - Programming Temporally Integrated Distributed Embedded Systems.
  • SPFSoS - Software Producibility for Systems of Systems, funded by the Naval Research Laboratory.
  • SwarmLab - Industrial interactions with Ericsson, IHI, NEC, Qualcomm, Sony, Toshiba and Visa.
  • TerrSwarm Research Center, funded by MARCO and DARPA, administered by SRC.
