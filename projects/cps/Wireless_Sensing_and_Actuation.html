Wireless Sensing and Actuation

Low-power wireless sensors and actuators enable much richer measurement and control of physical processes than what is affordable with wired systems. Issues that arise with wireless sensing and actuation include at least:
  • data models for distributed sensor data;
  • localization (positioning in space) of sensors and actuators; and
  • time synchronization, which enables coordinated sleep and wakeup times, and also enables time-stamping of sensor data with globally meaningful time stamps.
