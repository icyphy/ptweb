Research Summaries

These research summaries give a snapshot of many of the research efforts within the Ptolemy Project. These summaries appear in the collection of research abstracts Industrial Relations Office Research Summary. The research summary is published once a year by the EECS Industrial & Public Relations Office, Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences Department, and the Electronics Research Laboratory of the University of California at Berkeley.
