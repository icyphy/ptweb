iCyPhy Mini-Workshop, February 14, 2019

This is an invitation only half-day workshop for sponsors and researchers in the iCyPhy Research Center (Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems) at UC Berkeley. The goal of the workshop is to explore synergies across projects. The workshop is being held the same day as the BEARS Symposium, which normally occupies the morning. Advance registration is required for both BEARS and the Mini-Workshop (separate registrations).
