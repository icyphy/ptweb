Eleventh Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference

Friday, October 16, 2015, Berkeley, California

 

The Eleventh Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference is planned for Friday. October 16, 2015 in Berkeley, California in the Bancroft Hotel Great Hall.

The Ptolemy project studies modeling, simulation, and design of concurrent, real-time, embedded systems. The focus is on assembly of concurrent components.

The Ptolemy Miniconference is an opportunity for research collaborators and Ptolemy users and extenders from industry, academia, and government to get together, present their work to the Ptolemy community, and hear about related research and results. It is typically held every two years.

In addition, the meeting acts as an annual meeting for the Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems.

Please register for the conference. Registration will close on Friday, October 2, 2015. In the past, the workshop has filled prior to the deadline, so we recommend registering early.
