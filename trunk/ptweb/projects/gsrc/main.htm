Gigascale Silicon Research Center

The Ptolemy group is participating in the Gigascale Silicon Research Center, which is funded in part by the Microelectronics Advanced Research Corporation (MARCO)

Edward A. Lee is one of the many Principal investigators.

The Gigascale Silicon Research Center is just getting under way.

The interim website can be found at http://www-cad.eecs.berkeley.edu
