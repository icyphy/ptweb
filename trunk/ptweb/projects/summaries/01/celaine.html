Embedded Systems Design and Implementation

Researchers: Elaine Cheong
Advisor:Edward A. Lee
The Ptolemy project studies modeling, simulation, and design of concurrent, real-time, embedded systems. The goal of this work is to study concepts and techniques for integration with Ptolemy II that can help developers design and implement embedded, real-time systems. These include programming language concepts, techniques for communications and networks, and improving the reusability and reconfigurability of software for embedded systems.
