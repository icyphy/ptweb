Vergil: User interface for Ptolemy II

Researchers: Stephen Neuendorffer
Advisor:Edward A. Lee

This work is focused on the design of a modular and extensible user interface toolkit for Ptolemy II that is rich and powerful, yet also intuitive and relatively simple to learn. Diva graphics framework for manipulating figures.

Although this work is an important part of Ptolemy II, it is intended to be sufficiently general to be applicable to other design tools, and to enable further research into visualization and design of component-based models.
