Embedded Systems Programmer/Analyst IV Position Available

The Ptolemy group is hiring a Programmer/Analyst IV to work on the MoBIES project.

We may have other positions available, if you are interested, send email to Professor Edward A. Lee at eal@eecs.berkeley.edu

Programmer/Analyst IV, $56,900 - $105,300 (The maximum starting salary would be likely be $81,100) Job 02-125-10

Description:

Design, implement and manage embedded systems research laboratory for the Model Based Integration of Embedded Software (MoBIES) project (http://ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu/projects/mobies) in association with the Software Enabled Control Project (SEC). The SEC project is an ongoing project (http://sec.eecs.berkeley.edu) that focuses on real time control systems that make heavy use of software with a particular focus on avionics systems. The PA III incumbent will act as the lab managers and the lab will focus on deeply networked embedded systems, networked smart sensors and actuators, and software and hardware enabling technologies.

Qualifications

Advanced level experience with embedded processors, included software design and development, interfacing microcomputers to sensors and actuators and creating device drivers is required. Fully functional level experience with real time software, including operating systems such as VxWorks and QNX is preferred. Fully functional level experience with Windows NT and PC hardware is preferrred. Fully functional level experience with Java, including threading and JVM internals, and a willingness to experiment with Java technologies is required. Entry level experience with component technology such as CORBA and RMI is preferred. Knowledge of IEEE 1451 and Bluetooth is a plus.
