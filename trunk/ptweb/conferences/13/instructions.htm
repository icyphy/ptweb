Notes for Presentations and Posters

Each standard presentation will be 20 minutes in length, including setup and questions.

Each poster group will have the opportunity to give a 90 second (including setup time) overview presentation.

Presentations

  • Powerpoint Presentation Template - If you would like, you can use this template for your presentation, but it not required that you use it.
    • If you are giving a 20 minute presentation, please note the following:

    To decrease set up time, it would help if all of the presentations were on our presentation laptops.

    We would like to make all the presentations available in a printed hand out and on the Ptolemy website as 2up PDF files and as Powerpoint files. Let us know if this would be a problem.

    If possible, please email your Powerpoint presentation to ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu by 6am Monday, November 4.

    The printed handout will go to the printers on Tuesday, November 5 If you do not make the Monday 6am deadline, then there is a good chance that the printed handout will not reflect your new materials.

    Of course, you are welcome to use your own laptop, though there will be the usual minor delay in switching between laptops.

    Posters

    We would like to include printed copies of the posters in the handout.
    Please email your poster to ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu by 6am Monday, November 4.

    We will provide one easel and one posterboard (30"x40", which will take nine 8.5" x 11" pieces of paper). If you must have a second board, please send mail to ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu and let us know by 6 am, Monday, November 4th

    You can either bring your own individual paper, or you may use the Powerpoint Poster Template.

    If you are a member of CHESS, you may print your poster using the CHESS large format printer.
    Please bring your poster with you on Thursday, November 7.

    You are responsible for printing off your poster and attaching it to the posterboard. We will have clips for your use.

    If you are preparing a poster, please note that there will be an opportunity to present a 90 second overview of your poster. You need not prepare slides, though having two or three slides is encouraged.

    We encourage you to give have a live demonstration at your poster. If you will be giving a demonstration, please let us know by noting this on the registration form or by emailing ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu so that we can provide power and a monitor.

    If you prepare overview slides, we strongly encourage you to email them to ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu by 6am Monday, November 4 so that we can easily switch between overviews in a timely fashion.

    We would like to make all the posters themselves available in a printed hand out and on the Ptolemy website as 2up PDF files and as Powerpoint files. Let us know if this would be a problem. We will not be making the overview slides available in the handout.

    If possible, please email your poster to ptconf13 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu by 6am Monday, November 4
