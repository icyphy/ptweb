About EMSOFT

The International Conference on Embedded Software (EMSOFT) brings together researchers and developers from academia, industry, and government to advance the science, engineering, and technology in embedded software development.

EMSOFT 2010 (the 10th in the series!) is part of Embedded Systems Week.

Please visit www.esweek.org for venue and travel information.

Important Dates

  • Abstract submission: Tuesday, April 6, 2010 (extended!)
  • Full paper submission: submission system closes on April 12, 9:00 a.m. GMT-12.
  • Rebuttal process:
    • Wed June 9: initial reviews sent to authors.
    • Authors to prepare a max. 300 word response.
    • Mon June 14, 11pm Pacific time: deadline to enter the response in the system.
  • Acceptance notification: Monday, June 28, 2010
  • Final version submission: Tuesday, August 10, 2010
    • You should have received email from ACM publications: if not, let the Chairs know
  • Conference: October 24-29, 2010 (Sun - Fri)
