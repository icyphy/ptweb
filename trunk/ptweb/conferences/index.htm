Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference

The Ptolemy Miniconference is an opportunity for users and researchers from industry, academia, and government to get together and present their work to the Ptolemy community. It is typically held every two years.

The Tenth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference will be held on Thursday, November 7, 2013 in Berkeley, California.

The Ninth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference was held on Wednesday, February 16, 2011 in Berkeley, California.

The Eighth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference was held on Thursday, April 16, 2009 in Berkeley, California.

The Seventh Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference was held on Tuesday, February 13, 2007 in Berkeley, California.

The Sixth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference was held on Thursday, May 12, 2005 in Berkeley, California.
