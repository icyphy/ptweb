Eighth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference Program

Below is the draft program. If you are presenting, the first name listed is assumed to be the presenter. If this is not the case, or if you have corrections, please email them to ptconf09 at ptolemy eecs berkeley edu.

The Eighth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference will be held on Thursday, April 16, 2009 at the University of California, Berkeley.

Depending on the number of attendees, we will be in one of two rooms:
Either the Wozniak Lounge, 4th Floor Soda Hall
or the Donald O. Pederson Center, 540 A/B Cory Hall
See the Venue Page for details.

Note that Cyber-Physical Systems Week is being held in San Francisco earlier in the week.


If you are presenting or giving a poster, please see the instructions.

Please register for the conference. Registration will close on April 10. In the past, the conference has sold out, so we recommend registering early.
