Eighth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference Program

The Eighth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference will be held on Thursday, April 16, 2009 at the University of California, Berkeley in the Wozniak Lounge, 4th Floor Soda Hall

Note that Cyber-Physical Systems Week is being held in San Francisco earlier in the week.

The previous day, we will hold a tutorial on Wednesday, April 15, 2009 for Java programmers interested in hands-on learning about Ptolemy.


If you are presenting or giving a poster, please see the instructions.

Please register for the conference. Registration will close on April 10. In the past, the conference has sold out, so we recommend registering early.
