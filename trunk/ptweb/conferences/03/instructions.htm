Notes for Presentations and Posters

Each standard presentation will be 20 minutes in length, including setup and questions.

Each poster group will have the opportunity to give a 3 minute overview presentation.

If you are presenting a talk or poster, then be sure to note this on the registration form so that we waive the conference fee.

Presentations

If you are giving a 20 minute presentation, please note the following:

To decrease set up time, it would help if all of the presentations were on our presentation laptops.

We would like to make all the presentations available in a printed hand out and on the Ptolemy website as 2up PDF files and as Powerpoint files. Let us know if this would be a problem.

If possible, please email your Powerpoint presentation to ptconf03@ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu by 6am Monday, May 5.

The printed handout will go to the printers on Tuesday May 6th. If you do not make the Monday 6am deadline, then there is a good chance that the printed handout will not reflect your new materials.

Of course, you are welcome to use your own laptop, though there will be the usual minor delay in switching between laptops.

Posters

We will provide two easels, two posterboards (30"x40", which will take nine 8.5" x 11" pieces of paper) and glue to attach your pages to the posterboards. You need not use both posterboards. You should bring your own pages to attach to the posterboard.

If you are preparing a poster, please note that there will be an opportunity to present a 3 minute overview of your poster. You need not prepare slides, though having two or three slides is encouraged.

If you prepare overview slides, we strongly encourage you to email them to ptconf03@ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu by 6am Monday, May 5 so that we can easily switch between overviews in a timely fashion.

We would like to make all the posters themselves available in a printed hand out and on the Ptolemy website as 2up PDF files and as Powerpoint files. Let us know if this would be a problem. We will not be making the overview slides available in the handout.

If possible, please email your poster to ptconf03@ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu by 6am Monday, May 5.
