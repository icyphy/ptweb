Conferences

The Ptolemy Miniconference is an opportunity for users and researchers from industry, academia, and government to get together and present their work to the Ptolemy community. It is typically held every two years.

    *The 4th biennial Miniconference will occur on March 22-23, 2001.

    Venue: The Claremont Hotel, Berkeley

    If you would like to propose a talk or poster to be given at the Miniconference, please send a title and brief abstract to:

    Edward A. Lee at eal@eecs.berkeley.edu

    Deadline for submissions is December 15, 2000
