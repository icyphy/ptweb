Fourth Biennial Ptolemy Miniconference

The Ptolemy Miniconference is an opportunity for users and researchers from industry, academia, and government to get together and present their work to the Ptolemy community. It is typically held every two years.

Date: Thursday and Friday, March 22-23, 2001.

We are accepting waitlisted registrations. There are also still some rooms available at the Conference Hotel Rate ($195/night).
Please See 'Registration Waitlist' in Table of Contents at left

The program includes invited speakers from organizations worldwide, plus members of the Ptolemy project describing current research at Berkeley. Topics include:

  • Models of Computation
  • Hybrid and Modal Modeling
  • Code Generation
  • Modeling Concurrency
  • Modeling of Wireless Networks
  • Modeling Optical Networks
  • Implementing Radar and Sonar Systems
  • Dataflow in Cadence's VCC
  • Process Networks from Matlab
  • Distributed Optimization
  • Distributed Synchronized Clocks
  • Debugging Ptolemy Applications
  • Software Practice
  • Ptolemy II Architecture
  • Tutorial on Ptolemy II
