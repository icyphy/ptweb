If you would like to park at the University, please see the EECS Department public parking directions. UC Berkeley has a regularly scheduled shuttle which travels through downtown Berkeley near Bart and then around campus.

Since there is not a lot of parking right on campus we would suggest that you try and catch the shuttle from one of its designated pickup areas.

To recover the cost of conducting the conference, we will be charging a fee of $150, payable at the conference or in advance, by credit card or check made payable to The UC Regents. The fee is waived for invited speakers and sponsors of the Ptolemy Project.
