Workshop on Model-Based Methods for Embedded Software Creation, Wednesday, 9/29

When and Where?

Wednesday 9/29
8:30AM - 5:15PM
UC Berkeley EECS Dept. 535 Cory Hall (Wang Room)
The primary webpage is at: http://www.dsic-web.net/meetings/mobames_ucb_sep99/index.html

Each participant will be given the opportunity to make a five minute position statement, so please come prepared to do that. Then we will roll up our sleves and get to work setting a research agenda.

There is parking directly across from Cory Hall. However, participants need to first go to the East Gate booth to pick up a parking permit. Just tell the attendant the name of the Workshop to receive the permit.

Directions: If you are coming from Highway 80 take the University Ave. exit. Drive up to the end of University Avenue and make a left turn on Oxford St. Then, turn right into Hearst Ave. Go up Hearst to Gayley, turn right. The East Gate is approximate 1/8 mile down Gayley on the right-hand side of the road. The East Gate entrance is not really marked but you will notice a two way driveway and the booth. Go to the booth, they will have your permit. The parking cost is $8.00 dollars.

There is also public parking near the university. See the EECS Department public parking directions. UC Berkeley has a regularly scheduled shuttle which travels through downtown Berkeley near Bart and then around campus.
