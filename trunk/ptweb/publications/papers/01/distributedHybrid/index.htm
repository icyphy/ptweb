Modeling Distributed Hybrid Systems in Ptolemy II

Jie Liu, Xiaojun Liu, and Edward A. Lee

2001 American Control Conference
June 25 - 27, 2001 Arlington, VA, USA pp. 4984-4985

ABSTRACT

In this embedded tutorial, we present Ptolemy II as a modeling and simulation environment for distributed hybrid systems. In Ptolemy II, a distributed hybrid system is specified as a hierarchy of models: an event-based top level and distributed islands of hybrid systems. Each hybrid system is in turn a hierarchy of continuous-time models and finite state machines.
