    Ptolemy Classic has been used to simulate wireless in various places There is a discussion about ATM at http://ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu/ptolemyclassic/atm.htm

    Jens Voigt did a simulation of a wireless network in Ptolemy Classic ptolemy/src/domains/de/contrib/demo/60_GHZ_Indoor

    There are also some wireless demos at http://ptolemy.eecs.berkeley.edu/ptolemyclassic/almagest/docs/user/html/de.doc3.html

    The links page on the Ptolemy website has some links to he WiNES project at Dresden University of Technology
