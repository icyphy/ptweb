Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems (CHESS)

This work is supported in part by the Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems (CHESS), which is funded by Information Technology Research (ITR) project from the National Science Foundation (NSF awards #0720882 (CSR-EHS: PRET), #0647591 (CSR-SGER), and #0720841 (CSR-CPS)), the U. S. Army Research Office (ARO #W911NF-07-2-0019), the U. S. Air Force Office of Scientific Research (MURI #FA9550-06-0312 and AF-TRUST #FA9550-06-1-0244), the Air Force Research Lab (AFRL), the State of California Micro Program, and the following companies: Agilent, Bosch, DGIST, National Instruments, and Toyota.

The California MICRO Program

This work is supported in part by the the California MICRO Program, Agilent and Microsoft.

MURI project on High-Confidence Design for Distributed Embedded Systems (HCDDES

This work is supported in part by the High-Confidence Design for Distributed Embedded Systems (HCDDES) Multidisciplinary University Research Initiative (MURI).

Rome Air Force Research Laboratory

The Extensible Systems Dynamics Framework is a collaboration between Rome Air Force Research Laboratory and the Ptolemy project.

U.S. Army Research Laborator (ARL)

This work is sponsored in part by the Army Research Laboratory project, "Scalable Composition of Subsystems," and was accomplished under Cooperative Agreement Number W911NF-07-2-0019.

AF-TRUST

This work was supported in part by the AF-TRUST (Air Force Team for Research in Ubiquitous Secure Technology for GIG/NCES), which receives support from the DAF Air Force Office of Scientific Research (#FA9550-06-1-0244) and the following organizations: the National Science Foundation (# CCF-0424422) Cisco, British Telecom, ESCHER, HP, IBM, iCAST, Intel, Microsoft, ORNL, Pirelli, Qualcomm, Sun, Symantec, Telecom Italia, and United Technologies.
