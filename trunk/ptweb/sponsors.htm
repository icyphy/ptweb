Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems (CHESS)

This work was supported in part by the Center for Hybrid and Embedded Software Systems (CHESS) at UC Berkeley, which receives support from the National Science Foundation (NSF awards #0720882 (CSR-EHS: PRET), #1035672 (CPS: PTIDES), and #0931843 (ActionWebs)), the U. S. Army Research Office (ARO #W911NF-07-2-0019), the U. S. Air Force Office of Scientific Research (MURI #FA9550-06-0312, the Air Force Research Lab (AFRL), the Multiscale Systems Center (MuSyC), one of six research centers funded under the Focus Center Research Program, a Semiconductor Research Corporation program, and the following companies: Bosch, National Instruments, Thales, and Toyota.

MURI project on High-Confidence Design for Distributed Embedded Systems (HCDDES)

This work is supported in part by the High-Confidence Design for Distributed Embedded Systems (HCDDES) Multidisciplinary University Research Initiative (MURI) (#FA9550-06-0312).

Rome Air Force Research Laboratory

The AFRL Extensible Modeling and Analysis Framework is a collaboration between Rome Air Force Research Laboratory and the Ptolemy project.

U.S. Army Research Laboratory (ARL)

This work is sponsored in part by the Army Research Laboratory project, "Scalable Composition of Subsystems," and was accomplished under Cooperative Agreement Number W911NF-07-2-0019.
